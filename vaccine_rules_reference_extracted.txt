                        Vaccine Rules Reference 
                        Manitoba Health Immunization Program 
                        Based on eligibility criteria updated December 2025 
                         
                        Sources: 
                        https://www.gov.mb.ca/health/publichealth/cdc/vaccineeligibility.html 
                        https://www.gov.mb.ca/health/publichealth/cdc/div/schedules.html 
                        Influenza (Flu)  (influenza) 
                        Annual seasonal flu vaccine for all Manitobans 6 months and older. 
                         
                        Min age: 6 months 
                        Max age: None 
                        Doses required: 1 per season 
                        Dose interval: N/A 
                        Repeat interval: 365 days (annual) 
                        Risk factors required: None - universally eligible 
                        Contraindications: None 
                        Pregnancy safe: Yes - recommended in pregnancy 
                         
                        Rules and Constraints 
                             -   next_due = date_given + 365 days. 
                             -   OVERDUE if past next_due. DUE_SOON if within 30 days. UP_TO_DATE otherwise. 
                             -   Children under 9 receiving flu vaccine for the first time clinically need 2 doses in their first season. 
                                 The rule engine applies 1-dose logic to all ages. 
                             -   Adults 65 and older are eligible for an enhanced high-dose formulation. 
                         
                        Source: vaccineeligibility.html, schedules.html 
                         
                         
                        DTaP-IPV-Hib  (dtap_ipv_hib) 
                        Routine infant series protecting against Diphtheria, Tetanus, Pertussis, Polio, and Hib. Given at 2, 4, 6, 
                        and 18 months. 
                         
                        Min age: 2 months 
                        Max age: 83 months (under 7 years) 
                        Doses required: 4 
                        Dose interval: 56 days (8 weeks) minimum 
                        Repeat interval: None - series only 
                        Risk factors required: None 
                        Contraindications: None 
                        Pregnancy safe: No 
                         
                        Rules and Constraints 
                             -   Hard age gates at both ends. 
                             -   Under 2 months = NOT_ELIGIBLE, reason: too young. 
                             -   84 months or older = NOT_ELIGIBLE, reason: too old. 
                             -   83 months is the last eligible month. 
                             -   Once eligible always eligible does NOT apply past the maximum age - this is a strict cutoff. 
                         
                        Source: vaccineeligibility.html 
                         
                         
                        Tdap-IPV  (tdap_ipv) 
                        Pre-school booster protecting against Tetanus, Diphtheria, Pertussis, and Polio. Given at 4 to 6 years. 
                         
                        Min age: 48 months (4 years) 
                        Max age: 83 months (under 7 years) 
                        Doses required: 1 
                        Dose interval: N/A 
                        Repeat interval: None 
                        Risk factors required: None 
                        Contraindications: None 
                        Pregnancy safe: No 
                         
                        Rules and Constraints 
                             -   Single pre-school booster dose only. 
                             -   Adults are NOT eligible for Tdap-IPV - they receive Tdap instead. 
                             -   Anyone outside the 48 to 83 month window returns NOT_ELIGIBLE. 
                         
                        Source: vaccineeligibility.html, schedules.html 
                         
                         
                        Tdap  (tdap) 
                        Tetanus, Diphtheria, and Pertussis booster for adolescents and adults. Repeated every 10 years. 
                        Recommended in every pregnancy. 
                         
                        Min age: 156 months (13 years - Grade 8/9) 
                        Max age: None 
                        Doses required: 1, repeated every 10 years 
                        Dose interval: N/A 
                        Repeat interval: 3650 days (10 years) 
                        Risk factors required: None 
                        Contraindications: None 
                        Pregnancy safe: Yes - recommended in every pregnancy 
                         
                        Rules and Constraints 
                             -   next_due = date_given + 3650 days. 
                             -   OVERDUE if past next_due. DUE_SOON if within 30 days. UP_TO_DATE with next_due and 
                                 days_until populated. 
                             -   Also eligible: adults born on or after January 1 1989 who missed the Grade 8/9 dose. 
                             -   Recommended in every pregnancy regardless of prior dose timing, optimally at 27 to 32 weeks 
                                 gestation. 
                         
                        Source: vaccineeligibility.html, schedules.html 
                         
                         
                        Pneumococcal Conjugate (Pneu-C-15)  (pneu_c_15) 
                        Routine paediatric pneumococcal vaccine. 3 doses at 2, 4, and 12 months. 
                         
                        Min age: 2 months 
                        Max age: 59 months (under 5 years) 
                        Doses required: 3 
                        Dose interval: 56 days (8 weeks) minimum 
                        Repeat interval: None - series only 
                        Risk factors required: None 
                        Contraindications: None 
                        Pregnancy safe: No 
                         
                        Rules and Constraints 
                             -   Children 24 to 59 months who missed earlier doses are eligible for 1 catch-up dose. 
                             -   Schedules started with Pneu-C-13 do not need to be restarted. 
                             -   High-risk children should receive Pneu-C-20 instead. 
                         
                        Source: vaccineeligibility.html, schedules.html 
                         
                         
                        Pneumococcal Conjugate (Pneu-C-20)  (pneu_c_20) 
                        For adults 65 and older (universally eligible) and adults 18 to 64 with a qualifying high-risk condition. One 
                        dose only. 
                         
                        Min age: 216 months (18 years) for risk-based; 65 years for universal eligibility 
                        Max age: None 
                        Doses required: 1 
                        Dose interval: N/A 
                        Repeat interval: None - one-time dose 
                        Risk factor overrides (age 18-64 only): has_diabetes, has_chronic_lung, has_heart_disease, 
                        has_chronic_kidney, has_chronic_liver, has_asplenia, is_immunocompromised, has_hiv - any one 
                        qualifies 
                        Contraindications: None 
                        Pregnancy safe: No 
                         
                        Rules and Constraints 
                             -   Age 65 and older: universally eligible, no risk factor required. 
                             -   Age 18 to 64: requires at least one risk override flag. Without one, NOT_ELIGIBLE with reason: 
                                 eligible at age 65. 
                             -   Under 18 with a risk factor: still NOT_ELIGIBLE with reason: too young 
                                 (risk_override_min_age_months = 216). 
                             -   One-time dose - a second dose of Pneu-C-20 is not recommended once received. 
                         
                        Source: vaccineeligibility.html 
                         
                         
                        Rotavirus (Rotarix)  (rotavirus) 
                        2-dose infant series. First dose must be given before 15 weeks of age. Both doses must be complete by 8 
                        months. 
                         
                        Min age: 1 month (approximately 6 weeks) 
                        Max age: 8 months (series must complete by this age) 
                        Doses required: 2 
                        Dose interval: 28 days (4 weeks) minimum 
                        Repeat interval: None - series only 
                        Risk factors required: None 
                        Contraindications: None 
                        Pregnancy safe: No 
                         
                        Rules and Constraints 
                             -   The first dose must be given before 15 weeks of age (approximately 3.5 months). 
                             -   If the infant has NOT started the series and is 15 weeks or older, returns NOT_ELIGIBLE with 
                                 reason: too old to start. 
                             -   This is an exception to the once-eligible-always-eligible principle. 
                             -   If dose 1 has already been given, the series may continue past 15 weeks provided both doses 
                                 complete by 8 months. 
                             -   The rule engine evaluates started and unstarted infants through separate code paths. 
                         
                        Source: vaccineeligibility.html 
                         
                         
                        MMRV  (mmrv) 
                        Combined Measles, Mumps, Rubella, and Varicella vaccine for children 12 months to 12 years. Routine 
                        doses at 12 months and 4 to 6 years. 
                         
                        Min age: 12 months 
                        Max age: 155 months (under 13 years) 
                        Doses required: 2 
                        Dose interval: 42 days (6 weeks) minimum 
                        Repeat interval: None - series only 
                        Contraindications: is_immunocompromised, is_pregnant 
                        Pregnancy safe: No - contraindicated (live vaccine) 
                         
                        Rules and Constraints 
                             -   Contraindication check runs before the age check. If is_immunocompromised or is_pregnant is set, 
                                 returns NOT_ELIGIBLE with reason: contraindicated, regardless of age. 
                             -   MMRV is not approved by Health Canada for individuals 13 years of age or older. Max age of 155 
                                 months enforces this. 
                             -   At age 13 and older the components must be given separately as MMR and Varicella. 
                         
                        Source: vaccineeligibility.html 
                         
                         
                        MMR  (mmr) 
                        Measles, Mumps, Rubella. The number of doses required depends on the patient's birth year cohort per 
                        Manitoba Health policy. 
                         
                        Min age: 12 months (routine) 
                        Max age: None 
                        Dose interval: 28 days (4 weeks) minimum between doses 
                        Repeat interval: None - series only 
                        Contraindications: is_immunocompromised, is_pregnant 
                        Pregnancy safe: No - contraindicated (live vaccine) 
                         
                        Rules and Constraints 
                             -   Contraindication check runs before all other logic. 
                             -   Born before 1970: 0 doses required. Status = UP_TO_DATE with reason: considered immune. 
                             -   Born 1970 to 1984: 1 dose required. 
                             -   Born 1985 or later: 2 doses required. 
                             -   Healthcare workers (is_healthcare_worker = true): always 2 doses regardless of birth year. 
                             -   Travel dose: infants 6 to 11 months travelling to a measles-endemic country may receive 1 dose, 
                                 but this does not count toward the routine schedule. 
                         
                        Source: vaccineeligibility.html 
                         
                         
                        Varicella (Chickenpox)  (varicella) 
                        2-dose chickenpox vaccine for individuals born in 2008 or later. The birth year cutoff is a hard rule. 
                         
                        Min age: 12 months 
                        Max age: None (for those born 2008 or later) 
                        Birth year minimum: 2008 
                        Doses required: 2 
                        Dose interval: 84 days (12 weeks) minimum 
                        Repeat interval: None - series only 
                        Contraindications: is_immunocompromised, is_pregnant 
                        Pregnancy safe: No - contraindicated (live vaccine) 
                         
                        Rules and Constraints 
                             -   Contraindication check runs before the birth year check. 
                             -   Born before 2008 = NOT_ELIGIBLE with reason: born 2008 or later. 
                             -   Those born before 2008 are assumed to have natural immunity from wild-type varicella exposure. 
                             -   Individuals born before 2008 with zero doses may be eligible via the Not Previously Immunized 
                                 schedule, which is not modeled in the current rules. 
                         
                        Source: vaccineeligibility.html 
                         
                         
                        Meningococcal Conjugate (Men-C-ACYW)  (men_c_acyw) 
                        Protects against 4 strains of meningococcal disease. Dose 1 at 12 months, dose 2 in Grade 6, 
                        approximately 9 years later. 
                         
                        Min age: 12 months (routine); 2 months with a qualifying risk factor 
                        Max age: None 
                        Doses required: 2 
                        Dose interval: 3285 days (~9 years) 
                        Repeat interval: None (boosters every 3 to 5 years for high-risk not modeled) 
                        Risk factor overrides: has_asplenia, has_hiv, is_immunocompromised - any one eligible from 2 months 
                        Contraindications: None 
                        Pregnancy safe: No 
                         
                        Rules and Constraints 
                             -   The 3285-day dose interval encodes the clinical gap between the 12-month dose and the Grade 6 
                                 booster. 
                             -   A patient who recently received dose 1 shows UP_TO_DATE with reason: dose 2 of 2 due. 
                             -   Status only becomes OVERDUE for dose 2 once 3285 days have elapsed since dose 1. 
                             -   High-risk individuals are eligible from 2 months of age (risk_override_min_age_months = 2). 
                         
                        Source: vaccineeligibility.html, schedules.html 
                         
                         
                        Hepatitis B  (hepatitis_b) 
                        Routine Grade 6 school program (2 doses). High-risk individuals of any age require 3 doses. Risk factors 
                        increase the dose count and lower the minimum eligible age to birth. 
                         
                        Min age (standard): 132 months (11 years - Grade 6) 
                        Min age (with risk factor): 0 months (from birth) 
                        Max age: None 
                        Doses required (standard): 2 
                        Doses required (with risk factor): 3 
                        Dose interval: 120 days (~4 months) minimum 
                        Risk factor overrides: has_chronic_liver, has_chronic_kidney, has_hiv, is_immunocompromised, 
                        has_asplenia - any one sets doses to 3 and min age to 0 
                        Contraindications: None 
                        Pregnancy safe: Yes 
                         
                        Rules and Constraints 
                             -   doses_required is not a fixed value - it is computed at runtime. 
                             -   If any risk_factor_override flag is present, doses_required becomes 3. 
                             -   Without a risk factor, doses_required remains 2. 
                             -   risk_override_min_age_months = 0 means high-risk individuals are eligible from birth, bypassing 
                                 the 132-month standard minimum age. 
                         
                        Source: vaccineeligibility.html, schedules.html 
                         
                         
                        Hepatitis A  (hepatitis_a) 
                        2-dose series for high-risk individuals 6 months and older. There is no base eligible population - a 
                        qualifying risk condition is mandatory. 
                         
                        Min age: 6 months 
                        Max age: None 
                        Doses required: 2 
                        Dose interval: 180 days (~6 months) minimum 
                        Repeat interval: None - series only 
                        Risk factors required: has_chronic_liver, has_chronic_kidney, has_hiv, is_immunocompromised - any 
                        one qualifies 
                        Risk factor overrides: None - risk_factors_required is the only entry point 
                        Contraindications: None 
                        Pregnancy safe: Yes 
                         
                        Rules and Constraints 
                             -   risk_factors_required is not empty, so there is no base eligible population. 
                             -   If no qualifying condition is present, returns NOT_ELIGIBLE with reason: only eligible with 
                                 qualifying risk factor. 
                             -   This differs from risk_factor_overrides - there is no default eligible group to expand from. 
                             -   Manitoba Health policy also lists homelessness, illicit drug use, and MSM as qualifying conditions. 
                                 The exact patient model field mapping should be verified in the rule engine. 
                             -   Once eligibility is confirmed, standard 2-dose series logic applies with the 180-day interval. 
                         
                        Source: vaccineeligibility.html 
                         
                         
                        Human Papillomavirus (HPV)  (hpv) 
                        Routine Grade 6 school program. 2 doses if first dose given before age 15; 3 doses if first dose at age 15 
                        or older. 
                         
                        Min age: 108 months (9 years) 
                        Max age: None 
                        Doses required (JSON default): 2 - computed dynamically at runtime 
                        Dose interval: 180 days (~6 months) minimum 
                        Repeat interval: None - series only 
                        Risk factors required: None - universally eligible above minimum age 
                        Contraindications: None 
                        Pregnancy safe: No 
                         
                        Rules and Constraints 
                             -   doses_required is computed from the patient's age at their FIRST DOSE, not their current age. 
                             -   First dose given before the patient's 15th birthday = 2 doses total. 
                             -   First dose given on or after the patient's 15th birthday = 3 doses total. 
                             -   A series started before age 15 stays at 2 doses even if the patient is now 16 or older. 
                             -   If no doses yet, current age is used to plan: under 15 plans for 2 doses; 15 and older plans for 3 
                                 doses. 
                             -   Routine school program: females born on or after January 1 1997 and males born on or after 
                                 January 1 2002. 
                         
                        Source: vaccineeligibility.html, schedules.html 
                         
                         
                        RSV Vaccine  (rsv) 
                        For adults aged 60 and older. Manitoba Health policy ties eligibility to personal care home residence or 
                        panel status, though the current rule engine triggers on age alone. 
                         
                        Min age: 720 months (60 years) 
                        Max age: None 
                        Doses required: 1 
                        Dose interval: N/A 
                        Repeat interval: 365 days (per vaccine_rules.json - see notes) 
                        Risk factors required: None - age 60+ triggers eligibility per rule engine 
                        Contraindications: None 
                        Pregnancy safe: No 
                         
                        Rules and Constraints 
                             -   The rule engine returns OVERDUE for any patient 60 or older with no RSV history. 
                             -   Manitoba Health policy requires at least one care-setting qualifier: resides_in_pch, is_pch_respite, 
                                 is_panelled_for_pch_in_transitional_care, or is_panelled_for_pch_in_chronic_care. 
                             -   The current vaccine_rules.json does not enforce these flags - age alone triggers OVERDUE. This is 
                                 a potential gap between the rule engine and Manitoba policy. 
                             -   The JSON specifies repeat_interval_days = 365 but Manitoba Health policy intends RSV as a one-
                                 time dose. This should be reviewed. 
                             -   Infant RSV: infants born October 1 2025 to March 31 2026 are eligible for nirsevimab. This is not 
                                 modeled in vaccine_rules.json. 
                         
                        Source: vaccineeligibility.html
